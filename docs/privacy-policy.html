Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ផ្ទះ ឆ្នោតយួន (Home Lottery) ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our Android application com.homelottery.app (the "App").

The App shows lottery results (Khmer, Vietnam, MinhNam, Thai and ThaiSou), daily number predictions and links to our live result streams. It does not sell lottery tickets or accept bets.

Information We Collect

The App does not require registration or login, and we do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details.

Information Sent When the App Connects to Our Server

To show results, predictions and in-app banners, the App downloads data from our server (bkhlottery.cc). These requests do not include any personal information or account. Like any website, our server and hosting provider may automatically receive standard technical information with each request, such as your IP address, the time of the request and basic device or app details. This information is used only to deliver the service, keep it secure and fix problems.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App can alert you when new lottery results are published and when a new version of the App is available. To do this, the App checks our server for new results in the background (about every 15 minutes, when you have an internet connection) and asks Google Play about updates. You can turn result alerts off in the App's menu, and you can turn all notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves a few settings on your device only, such as your chosen language, whether result alerts are on, and which update or result you have already been notified about. These settings are not sent to us and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

Advertising

The App shows banner and pop-up images that we manage ourselves and load from our server. We do not use third-party advertising networks, and we do not use advertising IDs or track you to show ads. If you tap a banner, it may open another website or app (for example, Telegram). Those services are run by other companies and have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Play services – The App uses the Google Play Integrity API to check that it is a genuine copy installed from Google Play, and the Google Play In-App Updates API to check for new versions. Google may process device information to provide these features. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube – The "Live Results on YouTube" screen loads our YouTube channel inside the App. YouTube (Google) may use cookies and collect information about your viewing, as described in the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

The App does not use any analytics or tracking services.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone, because we do not collect it.

Data Security

All connections between the App and our server use encrypted HTTPS.

Age Requirement

The App contains lottery-related content and is intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
